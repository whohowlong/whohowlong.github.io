Computer vision · Multimodal learning

I earned a B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Minnan Normal University in 2025. As an undergraduate research assistant, I worked on visual object tracking and published a first-author survey on multimodal emotion recognition.

My experience also includes quantitative development, AI education, and a vision project for an assembly workstation. I’m interested in making visual perception and multimodal models more reliable under occlusion and noise.

I’m looking for research assistant opportunities in computer vision or multimodal learning. I’m available remotely or in person in Nanjing.

Selected projects

Undergraduate research · Occlusion and Transformer-based tracking

Visual object tracking

When an object is occluded, unreliable observations can affect model updates and cause later predictions to drift.

I contributed code, data processing, and experiments to the occlusion study. For the subsequent Transformer tracking study, I contributed to experiments and verification. The published contribution statement credits me with Validation.

Tracking paper ↗

HKUST(GZ) Red Bird Camp · 2026 team project

Gongyou Zhiban · Vision-assisted assembly

We designed a camera-based workflow to recognize assembly stages and flag missed, incorrect, or out-of-order operations in small-batch assembly.

I helped design the logic for recognizing assembly stages, flagging errors, and asking the operator for confirmation. I also specified PC-side training, ONNX export, and the handoff to the teammate responsible for edge deployment.

Research and practice

Computer vision research

2022–2024 · Undergraduate lab
